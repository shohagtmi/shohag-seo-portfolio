Services

SEO services built around what actually moves the needle.

Eight services, each solving a specific problem — not a bundled package you don't need.

The Work

Eight services, each solving one problem

01 — Audit

SEO Audit

Problem it solves: You don't know why your site isn't ranking, or where to start.

What's included:

  • Full technical crawl (indexation, speed, mobile usability)
  • On-page and content review
  • Prioritized list of fixes, ranked by impact
02 — Research

Keyword Research

Problem it solves: You're guessing what your customers search for, or targeting terms with no real intent behind them.

  • Keyword clusters mapped to buyer intent
  • Competitor keyword gaps
  • A realistic priority list, not a 500-keyword dump
03 — On-Page

On-Page SEO

Problem it solves: Pages exist but aren't structured to rank or convert.

  • Title tags, meta descriptions, and heading structure
  • Internal linking improvements
  • Content-to-intent alignment
04 — Technical

Technical SEO

Problem it solves: Good content that search engines can't properly crawl or index.

  • Indexation and crawl budget review
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Structured data implementation
05 — Content

Content Optimization

Problem it solves: Existing content isn't performing despite being well-written.

  • Search-intent gap analysis
  • Readability and structure improvements
  • Refresh recommendations for aging content
06 — Local

Local SEO

Problem it solves: You're invisible for "near me" and local-intent searches.

  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Local citation and NAP consistency review
  • Location page structure for multi-location businesses
07 — Research

Competitor Analysis

Problem it solves: You don't know why competitors outrank you.

  • Content and keyword gap comparison
  • Technical and backlink benchmarking
  • Clear opportunities you can act on
08 — Ongoing

Monthly SEO Management

Problem it solves: SEO needs consistent attention, not a one-time fix.

  • Monthly reporting tied to real metrics
  • Ongoing content and technical improvements
  • Direct access for questions between reports
