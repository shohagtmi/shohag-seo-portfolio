Who I am

My name is Monjurul Islam Shohag. I go by Shohag professionally. I'm an SEO Specialist and Content Strategist based in Dhaka, currently focused on building a solid, honest track record through real projects rather than exaggerated claims.

Why I chose SEO

I was drawn to SEO because it sits at the intersection of technical thinking, writing, and business strategy. It rewards patience and precision — small, correct changes compound into real results over time. That combination of logic and language is what keeps me interested.

My approach to SEO

I don't believe in SEO as a checklist. Every recommendation should trace back to a real reason: a crawl error blocking indexation, a content gap versus a competitor, a page that doesn't match search intent. If I can't explain why something matters, I don't recommend it just to look thorough.

What I focus on

Right now, my focus is on getting the fundamentals right: technical audits, keyword research grounded in real search intent, and on-page structure that both search engines and readers can follow. I'd rather do fewer things well than offer everything at a surface level.

Learning mindset

SEO changes constantly — algorithm updates, the rise of AI-driven search, shifting user behavior. I treat learning as part of the job, not a side activity: testing tools, reading documentation directly from Google, and studying how AI is changing how people search.

Working philosophy

Honest timelines. Clear communication. No vanity metrics dressed up as results. If something isn't working, I'll say so early rather than let a report hide it.
