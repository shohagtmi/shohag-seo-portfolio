Case Study

Agriculture Website — SEO Project

A personal project used to practice and demonstrate a complete SEO workflow, from research to implementation.

Overview

Project Overview

Industry: Agriculture

My role: SEO (keyword research, on-page, technical)

Objective: Build and optimize an agriculture-niche website from the ground up, applying a real SEO process rather than a theoretical one.

Situation

Initial Situation

The site started as a basic build with no SEO structure in place — no keyword targeting, no metadata strategy, and no technical review.

Research

SEO & Keyword Research

  • Identified core topics relevant to the agriculture niche
  • Mapped search intent for target terms
  • Reviewed competing pages for content gaps

This followed the same keyword research process described in my services.

On-Page

On-Page Work

  • Wrote title tags and meta descriptions for key pages
  • Structured headings around target topics
  • Improved internal linking between related pages
Technical

Technical SEO

  • Checked indexability and crawlability
  • Reviewed page speed and mobile usability
  • Verified clean, descriptive URL structure

Full breakdown of what this involves: Technical SEO service.

Content

Content Strategy

Content was planned around the identified keyword clusters, prioritizing pages that matched clear search intent over broad, generic topics.

Outcome

Outcome

Overall result to date: good — the on-page and technical foundation is in place.

Traffic & ranking data: coming soon
Learnings

What I Learned

Working on a full site end to end reinforced how much of SEO impact depends on getting the technical and structural basics right before content or link-building matters at all.

Next

Future Improvements

  • Set up Search Console and Analytics tracking for measurable results
  • Expand content around secondary keyword clusters
  • Add structured data for relevant page types
